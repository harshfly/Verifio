Common Questions

What file formats are supported?

We currently support PDF, DOCX, and plain text files. Scanned PDFs are processed with OCR fallback for text extraction.

How accurate is AI detection?

Our dual-signal approach (RoBERTa + perplexity heuristics) achieves high accuracy, but outputs are always labelled as probabilities — never binary verdicts.

Can I adjust the scoring weights?

Yes — Enterprise plans include admin access to configure module weights, allowing your institution to prioritize the signals that matter most.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All files are encrypted at rest and in transit. Role-based access ensures reports are only visible to authorized users.
